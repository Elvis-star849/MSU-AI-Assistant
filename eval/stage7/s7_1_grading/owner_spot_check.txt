---- N5-a | Can I switch from an MPhil to a PhD, and when?
Yes – you may upgrade from an MPhil to a PhD/DPhil.
The upgrade can be requested after the student has completed at least 12 months of MPhil study and the supervisor(s) recommend the change. The application must be accompanied by a written recommendation from the Faculty Higher‑Degrees Committee (or the relevant School/Institute) and submitted to the Research and Innovation Division. The DCAA will review the application and recommend it to Senate, which will then approve the upgrade and prescribe any additional study required for the PhD/DPhil.

The MPhil must be finished within 24 months of full registration, and the PhD/DPhil within 48 months after full registration, but the upgrade itself is governed by the 12‑month rule and the recommendation process described above. (MSU Humanities Yearbook p. 151; MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2 p. 154)
