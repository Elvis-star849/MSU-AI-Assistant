### 1. How many notional hours make up one credit?

One credit is equal to 10 notional hours.
This is stated in the definitions of the General Academic Regulations:

"Credit" An educational award which in accumulation with other educational awards leads to the award of a degree/diploma certificate. One credit shall consist of 10 notional hours." (MSU Humanities Yearbook, p. 124; MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, p. 125)

### 2. From which university will the first two cohorts of MSU MBChB students graduate?

The first two MBChB cohorts at MSU will graduate with degrees from the University of Zimbabwe, and they follow the UZ MBChB regulations.
This is stated in the preamble to the MBChB regulations:

"With the approval of the University of Zimbabwe (UZ) and the Medical and Dental Professions Council of Zimbabwe, the first two cohorts of MBChB students from the Midlands State University (MSU) will graduate with degrees from the University of Zimbabwe." (MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, p. 741)

### 3. What is the minimum age and work experience required for the MBA programme?

An MBA applicant must normally be at least 25 years old and have at least two years of relevant working experience.
This is stated in the MBA entry requirements:

"To be eligible for admission to the MBA Programme, a candidate must normally: Be at least 25 years of age … Have at least two (2) years relevant working experience." (MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, pp. 613–614)

### 4. Which Act made MSU Zimbabwe's third state university, and when was it passed?

The State University in the Midlands Act of April 1999 turned the Gweru devolution project into Zimbabwe's third state university.
This is stated in the Historical Note:

"…the Minister of Higher Education and Technology transformed the devolution project at Gweru into Zimbabwe's third state university by means of the State University in the Midlands Act of April 1999." (MSU Humanities Yearbook, p. 75; MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, p. 75)

### 7. How is the Master of Commerce in Forensic Auditing and Accounting assessed, and what is the pass mark?

Continuous assessment counts for 40% and the final exam for 60%. A student needs an aggregate of 50% and must also score at least 50% in the exam.
This is stated in the programme regulations:

"Continuous assessment shall account for 40% of the overall assessment, while the final examination will account for 60% of the overall assessment. The aggregate pass mark shall be 50% and the candidate must have obtained at least 50% in the examination." (MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, p. 412)

### 8. What mark range gives an Upper Second Class (2.1) degree?

An Upper Second Class (2.1) degree is awarded for marks of 65% to 74%.
This is stated in the Grading and Degree Classification System:

"75% and above – 1 (First Class); 65%–74% – 2.1 (Upper Second Class); 60%–64% – 2.2 (Lower Second Class); 50%–59% – 3 (Pass); 49% and below – Fail" (MSU Humanities Yearbook, p. 131; MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, p. 132)

### 41. What must a student do if they cannot attend classes for health reasons?

If the absence lasts longer than 72 hours, the student must notify the Faculty Officer as soon as possible. They must also submit a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

### 42. Can a student with a Professional Diploma in Data Science finish the BCom Honours in Data Science and Informatics in two levels?

No. Such a student may enter directly into Level 2, but no candidate can finish in fewer than three academic levels, and Work-Related Learning at Level 3 is compulsory.
This is stated in the Special Entry provisions:

"Candidates who have successfully completed a Professional Diploma in Data Science or have obtained equivalent qualifications within three years may apply for direct entry into Level 2 of the degree programme … No candidate may complete the degree in less than three academic levels." (MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2, p. 466)
