# MSU RAG Evaluation Set - 50 Questions

Page numbers are **PDF page numbers** (as shown in a PDF viewer), not the printed page numbers.


## HUMANITIES - General Information and Regulations (2024-2028)

**Q1.** Who chaired the Committee on the Devolution of Bachelor of Education and Bachelor of Technology Degree Programmes appointed in October 1995?  
**Answer:** Professor C.J. Chetsanga (appointed by the then Minister of Higher Education and Technology, Hon. Dr S.I. Mudenge).  
*Source: PDF p.74 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q2.** Which Act transformed the Gweru devolution project into Zimbabwe's third state university, and when was it passed?  
**Answer:** The State University in the Midlands Act of April 1999.  
*Source: PDF p.75 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q3.** Who was the first Vice-Chancellor of Midlands State University and from when did the appointment take effect?  
**Answer:** Professor Ngwabi Bhebe, a historian, with effect from December 1999.  
*Source: PDF p.75 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q4.** Who was the Chairperson of the inaugural Council of Midlands State University?  
**Answer:** Mr C.F. Dube of Dube Manikai and Hwacha Legal Practitioners.  
*Source: PDF p.75 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q5.** How many hectares of State land did the Vungu Rural District Council grant the University?  
**Answer:** 164 hectares (the land stretches westwards from the former Gweru Teachers College).  
*Source: PDF p.75-76 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q6.** Where is the Zvishavane Campus located and which faculties does it host?  
**Answer:** At the old Shabanie Mine Office Complex; it hosts the Faculty of Engineering and Geosciences and the Faculty of Arts and Humanities.  
*Source: PDF p.76 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q7.** Which research institute is hosted at the Zvishavane Campus and in what year was it established?  
**Answer:** The Tugwi-Mukosi Multi-Disciplinary Research Institute, established in 2018.  
*Source: PDF p.76 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q8.** According to the General Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Degrees, how many notional hours make up one credit?  
**Answer:** 10 notional hours.  
*Source: PDF p.124 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q9.** How long is a semester and how is it divided?  
**Answer:** Normally 15 weeks: 13 weeks of teaching and two weeks of examinations.  
*Source: PDF p.124 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q10.** Once a student has been required to 'withdraw' from the University, how long must they wait before applying for admission again?  
**Answer:** Four successive semesters must elapse.  
*Source: PDF p.124 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q11.** What are the general minimum entry requirements for an undergraduate degree?  
**Answer:** Passes in at least 5 subjects at Ordinary Level and at least two subjects at Advanced Level (or equivalents), including English Language at 'O' level with a C or better.  
*Source: PDF p.126 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q12.** What percentage range corresponds to an Upper Second Class (2.1) undergraduate degree?  
**Answer:** 65% - 74%. (First Class is 75% and above, 2.2 is 60-64%, Pass is 50-59%, and 49% and below is a Fail.)  
*Source: PDF p.131 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q13.** Unless Senate approves otherwise, how much does continuous assessment contribute to the overall assessment of an undergraduate module?  
**Answer:** Between 25% and 50%.  
*Source: PDF p.131 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q14.** What minimum number of credits must undergraduate and Masters students attain to graduate?  
**Answer:** 120 credits for undergraduates and 90 credits for Masters (subject to faculty specifications).  
*Source: PDF p.132 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q15.** What is the minimum mark needed to qualify for a supplementary examination, and what is the maximum mark that can be awarded in it?  
**Answer:** A supplementable mark of at least 40% overall is needed; the maximum mark awarded is 50% (graded Pass/Fail, and continuous assessment is not included).  
*Source: PDF p.135 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q16.** For levels other than the Work-Related Learning level, what is the maximum number of modules a student may carry over to the next level?  
**Answer:** No more than 25% of the number of modules normally scheduled in that level of the programme.  
*Source: PDF p.135 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q17.** Within what period must a student affected by extenuating circumstances apply to their Faculty after missing an examination?  
**Answer:** Within one week of the missed examination.  
*Source: PDF p.134 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q18.** If an undergraduate student cannot attend classes for health reasons, after how long must they notify the Faculty Officer, and what must they submit?  
**Answer:** If absent for longer than 72 hours; they must notify the Faculty Officer as soon as possible and submit a certificate from a medical practitioner registered under the Medical, Dental and Allied Health Professions Act.  
*Source: PDF p.130 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q19.** What is the minimum notice period between a student receiving a summons and appearing before the Student Disciplinary Committee?  
**Answer:** Not less than five (5) days after the date the notice is received.  
*Source: PDF p.111 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q20.** What standard of proof must the Student Disciplinary Committee apply before finding that a student breached the Rules of Student Conduct and Discipline?  
**Answer:** Beyond reasonable doubt.  
*Source: PDF p.111 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q21.** After full registration, within what period must MPhil and PhD/DPhil students complete their studies?  
**Answer:** MPhil within 24 months (2 years); PhD/DPhil within 48 months (4 years). Continuation beyond that needs Senate approval.  
*Source: PDF p.150 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q22.** What are the age requirements for Mature Entry into a Bachelor's degree programme?  
**Answer:** At least 25 years for males and 23 years for females on the first day of the academic year (plus five approved 'O' Levels including English and Mathematics, normally having left full-time education at least five years earlier).  
*Source: PDF p.251-252 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q23.** For the Master of Arts in Forensic Archaeology (MAFA), how many modules are there, how are credits allocated, and how is it assessed?  
**Answer:** 11 modules; each worth 20 credits except the dissertation (90 credits); at least 290 credits required. Continuous assessment is 30% and end-of-semester exams 70% (three-hour exam per module); the dissertation is 100%.  
*Source: PDF p.174 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q24.** In the Master of Education in Lifelong Learning, how many credits is each module worth, how many credits are required, and how is the dissertation assessed?  
**Answer:** 13 modules of 18 credits each plus a 90-credit dissertation, for 306 credits. The dissertation is assessed 70% on the written research report and 30% on the oral defence (taught modules are 40% CA / 60% exam).  
*Source: PDF p.466-467 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q25.** How is the dissertation assessed in the Bachelor of Science Honours Degree in Social Work?  
**Answer:** Research project 75% and oral presentation 25%. (Coursework is 40% of the module mark - tests 50% and assignments 50% - and written exams are 60%.)  
*Source: PDF p.899-900 | Difficulty: medium*


## SCIENCES - General Information and Regulations Vol 2 (2024-2028)

**Q26.** What is the duration of the BSc Honours in Computer Science (HCSC) programme?  
**Answer:** 4 years full-time, or 3 years for the visiting programme.  
*Source: PDF p.895 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q27.** What are the normal entry 'A' Level requirements for the BSc Honours in Computer Science (HCSC)?  
**Answer:** At least 2 'A' Level passes in Mathematics AND Physics or Computer Science or another relevant science subject, plus five 'O' Levels including English, Mathematics and a Science subject at grade C or better.  
*Source: PDF p.895 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q28.** What are the entry requirements for the Visiting School/Block Release route into BSc Honours Computer Science?  
**Answer:** At least an ND or HND in Software Engineering or a relevant field, AND proof of employment in the field specifying the nature of duties.  
*Source: PDF p.895 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q29.** What is the Maximum MBKs Credit Load for the BSc Honours in Computer Science (HCSC)?  
**Answer:** 398 (Maximum credit load 540; minimum 480).  
*Source: PDF p.895 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q30.** From which university will the first two cohorts of MSU MBChB students receive their degrees?  
**Answer:** The University of Zimbabwe (UZ), with the approval of UZ and the Medical and Dental Professions Council of Zimbabwe. MSU students follow the UZ MBChB regulations.  
*Source: PDF p.741 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q31.** What 'A' Level subjects are required for admission to the MBChB programme?  
**Answer:** Chemistry plus any two of Biology (or Zoology), Mathematics and Physics. The subject not taken at A Level must normally have been passed at 'O' Level. English Language and Mathematics are required at 'O' Level.  
*Source: PDF p.742 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q32.** How long are the rural field attachments for MBChB students and in which years are they done?  
**Answer:** Four weeks in each of the second, third and final years.  
*Source: PDF p.742 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q33.** When is the MBChB Part V examination held and which subjects does it cover?  
**Answer:** In July; it covers Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Medicine, Paediatrics and Surgery (with Clinical Pathology built into these clinical subjects).  
*Source: PDF p.742 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q34.** What subject is examined in MBChB Part I, and when?  
**Answer:** Biochemistry, in or about the 14th week of the second semester of the first year.  
*Source: PDF p.742 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q35.** What is the programme code, duration and credit load of the Master of Commerce in Forensic Auditing and Accounting?  
**Answer:** Code BS16; duration 1.5 years; credit load 306 (minimum 270, maximum 340), ZNQF Level 9.  
*Source: PDF p.411 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q36.** In the Master of Commerce in Forensic Auditing and Accounting, how are coursework and the exam weighted, and what is needed to pass?  
**Answer:** Continuous assessment 40% and final examination 60%. The aggregate pass mark is 50%, and the candidate must also get at least 50% in the examination.  
*Source: PDF p.412 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q37.** Which first degrees qualify a candidate for the Master of Commerce in Forensic Auditing and Accounting?  
**Answer:** A first degree in Accounting, Forensic Accounting, Auditing, Banking and Finance, or any other related degree.  
*Source: PDF p.411 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q38.** What is the minimum age and work experience required for admission into the MBA (BLEAD01) programme?  
**Answer:** At least 25 years of age and at least two years of relevant working experience. Two references and an interview by the Faculty Post Graduate Studies Committee are also required.  
*Source: PDF p.613 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q39.** How many modules must an MBA candidate pass, and how many contact hours does each module have?  
**Answer:** A minimum of 15 modules (13 core and 2 non-core); each module has 60 contact hours.  
*Source: PDF p.613-614 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q40.** How long should the MBA dissertation be, and how many hard-bound copies must be submitted?  
**Answer:** About 20,000 words; three hard-bound copies, one each for the University Library, the GSBL Office and the student.  
*Source: PDF p.614 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q41.** What is the maximum duration allowed for completing the MBA programme?  
**Answer:** Four academic years (the minimum is two academic years full-time).  
*Source: PDF p.614 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q42.** What is the duration and credit load of the Bachelor of Engineering Honours in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (ENG01)?  
**Answer:** 5 years; credit load 627 (minimum 600, maximum 660).  
*Source: PDF p.636 | Difficulty: easy*

**Q43.** What are the Special Entry requirements for the BEng Honours in Electrical and Electronic Engineering?  
**Answer:** At least 5 'O' Levels including Mathematics, English and Combined Science, and at least a National Diploma in an approved Engineering field or a recognised equivalent. Candidates may be invited for an interview or entrance exam.  
*Source: PDF p.636 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q44.** What are the entry requirements and duration of the Master of Science in Crop Science (MCS)?  
**Answer:** At least a good first degree in Agronomy, Crop Science, Horticulture or a related field from a recognised university; duration 2.5 years.  
*Source: PDF p.257 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q45.** What are the assessment weightings for the Master of Science in Crop Science?  
**Answer:** Continuous assessment 30-50% and final exam 50-70%.  
*Source: PDF p.259 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q46.** What are the normal entry 'A' Level requirements for the BSc Honours in Food Science and Technology (FST)?  
**Answer:** At least 2 'A' Level passes, including Chemistry, Biology or Food Science, and one subject from Physics, Mathematics or Agriculture.  
*Source: PDF p.1097 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q47.** What is the minimum degree class required for normal entry into the Master of Science in Software Engineering, and how long is the programme?  
**Answer:** An Honours degree in Software Engineering or an equivalent computing science or engineering qualification with at least a 2.1; the programme lasts 18 months.  
*Source: PDF p.958 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q48.** What are the normal entry 'A' Level requirements for the Bachelor of Commerce Honours in Data Science and Informatics (BS08)?  
**Answer:** A pass in 'A' Level Mathematics or Statistics, plus passes in at least two of Accounts, Economics, Management of Business and Computing (Computer Studies/Software Engineering).  
*Source: PDF p.465 | Difficulty: medium*

**Q49.** Under Special Entry for the BCom Honours in Data Science and Informatics, which candidates can enter Level 2 directly, and what limit applies?  
**Answer:** Candidates who completed a Professional Diploma in Data Science (or equivalent) within three years can enter Level 2 directly, subject to places. No candidate may finish in fewer than three academic levels, and Work-Related Learning at Level 3 is compulsory.  
*Source: PDF p.466 | Difficulty: hard*

**Q50.** What are the entry requirements and duration of the Master of Science in Optic Fibre and Photonic Engineering (MOF)?  
**Answer:** At least a second-class first degree in Physics, Applied Physics or another materials-science-related degree from a university recognised by MSU; duration 2 years (minimum credit load 336).  
*Source: PDF p.1086-1087 | Difficulty: medium*
