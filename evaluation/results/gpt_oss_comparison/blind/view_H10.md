**Entry requirements for the 3‑year (Weekend School) route of the BSc Honours in Industrial Sociology**

- At least five ‘O’ level passes, including English Language.  
- Must be a holder of a Diploma, HND, or any related qualification in any of the above disciplines.  
- Must be employed in a field related to the above disciplines.  
- Must submit a confirmation letter of employment from the employer.
