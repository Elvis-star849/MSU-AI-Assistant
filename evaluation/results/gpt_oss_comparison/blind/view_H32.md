**Entry requirements for the Master of Science in Human Capital Management (MHCD)**  

- **Normal entry**: An Honours degree in human resource management, Human Resource Development or Employment Relations, or any relevant social‑science degree or qualification that has been obtained with a pass of an upper second class (2.1) or better. [Evidence 1]
