## Answer H24-6dc704

*Automatic hints (not grades):* {"cited": [1, 3, 4], "out_of_range": [], "cites_any": true}

**Current (2027 intake) entry requirements** – as stated in the MSU January/February 2027 Intake Advert (page 10)  
- **A‑level**: a pass in *A‑level Mathematics* or *Statistics* and *any other A‑level subject* (or an ND/HND in an Information Technology‑related field, or any other relevant qualification, or confirmation of employment in the field of IT).  
- **O‑level**: at least **5 O‑level passes** at grade C or better, **including English Language**, in addition to the specified A‑level subjects or diplomas.  

*Source: Evidence 3, page 10.*

---

**Earlier (yearbook) entry requirements** – as recorded in the MSU Sciences Yearbook Vol. 2 (pages 465‑466)  
- **A‑level**: a pass in *A‑level Mathematics* or *Statistics* **and** a pass at A‑level in **at least two** of the following subjects (or their equivalents): Accounts, Economics, Management of Business, Computing (Computer Studies/Software Engineering).  
- **O‑level**: must satisfy the minimum conditions prescribed under the General Regulations for English and Mathematics at O‑level.  

*Source: Evidence 1, page 465; Evidence 4, page 466.*

---

**Difference**  
- The 2027 advert broadens the A‑level requirement to any other A‑level subject (or an IT‑related diploma/qualification or employment confirmation) rather than the specific set of subjects listed in the yearbook.  
- The yearbook specifies that two of the listed subjects are required, whereas the advert does not impose that restriction.  
- Both documents require the standard O‑level English and Mathematics conditions, but the advert explicitly states the minimum of five O‑level passes at grade C or better, including English.  

*Sources: Evidence 3 (current) vs. Evidence 1 & 4 (older).*
