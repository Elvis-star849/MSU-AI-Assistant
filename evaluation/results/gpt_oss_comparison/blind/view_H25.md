**Differences**

- The **2027 advert** (Evidence 3) requires only a *relevant Bachelor’s degree* together with a *special Honours or certificate* in Monitoring and Evaluation; it does **not** mention the need for an Honours degree, a postgraduate diploma, or work‑experience considerations.
- The **yearbook regulation** (Evidence 1) specifies a higher academic threshold: an *Honours degree* (in Monitoring and Evaluation or Development Studies) **or** a *first degree plus a Postgraduate Diploma* in a related discipline, and notes that work experience is advantageous.

**Current requirement**

According to rule 9, the 2027 intake advert is the most recent and therefore the current entry requirement: a relevant Bachelor’s degree plus a special Honours or certificate in Monitoring and Evaluation (plus the general O‑level requirement).
