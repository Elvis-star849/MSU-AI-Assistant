**Entry requirements for the Master of Commerce Degree in Strategic Marketing**

Applicants should have a good honours degree (first or upper second) in one of the following fields:  
- Marketing  
- Business Management  
- Tourism and Hospitality Management  
- Insurance and Risk Management  
- Economics and Entrepreneurship  

or any other relevant degree programme.
