**Applicant:** What about mature entry?

**Assistant:**

Mature entry at MSU. The university's general regulations say that applicants who are at least 25 (men) or 23 (women) on the first day of the academic year, and who do not qualify through normal or special entry, may apply if they have at least five O Level passes including English Language (and Mathematics where required) and can show they are suited to university study through their attainments and/or relevant work experience. Interviews or tests may be required. (MSU Humanities Yearbook, General Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Degrees, 3.3 Mature Entry, p. 129; the yearbook regulations may be older than the 2027 advert.)

The 2027 intake advert lists mature entry for 18 programmes, some with extra conditions (for example five years of relevant experience, an O Level Science pass, or current employment). Age alone is not enough. For programmes where the advert does not mention mature entry, please ask the Admissions Office whether it is possible.
